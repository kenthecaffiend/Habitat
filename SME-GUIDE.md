# Owner guide

## People and access

Administrators add employees, maintain their employment/pay settings and archive departures. Archiving preserves earlier records and excludes archived people from ordinary future payroll selection. Employees see their own records; they can edit their address/postal code and change their own password through the authenticated form. Administrative access and colleague-account activation are separate controls.

## Leave and claims

Set current-year remaining annual leave and valid carryover with an effective date. Existing remaining balances are opening facts, not a reason to deduct imported historical usage twice. Administrators can make auditable corrections. Requests retain normal review, overlap and balance checks. Medical evidence is optional. Imported history is labelled separately and is not automatically a new operational approval.

Claims accept PNG/JPEG/HEIC receipts, up to five files and 20MB per file. Files are private and ownership-checked. Eligible claim edits may retain, remove or replace receipts; edits requiring review return to pending. Allocated/paid claims remain protected from silent changes or duplicate reimbursement.

## Payroll and payslips

1. Save company details and each employee's effective payroll settings once. Review supported CPF/contribution applicability; race alone is not a complete contribution decision.
2. Choose the payroll period and employees, adding only applicable additional wages. Review automatically captured eligible approved unpaid claims. Claims are reimbursements, not silently treated as CPF wages.
3. Review the draft and finalise it. Applicable pay amounts remain locked while an unpaid draft/payslip exists. Discard is recoverable and limited to supported unissued history.
4. After making payment through the normal payment channel, record the run's actual payment date. Habitat generates the included employees' payslip PDFs; failed documents show a retry state without undoing the recorded payment.
5. Employees download their own current payslips. An administrator may void a document and generate a new immutable replacement; this does not undo payment. Original versions remain in administrative history.

Do not fabricate missing opening wage/leave information. Use supported review/correction paths. Recording, finalising or issuing genuine records requires an accepted installation and accurate user-entered facts.

## Recovery and readiness

Back up the database and private files together using the installation's verified procedure. Keep encrypted off-host copies and recovery keys separately, and test restoration of a populated copy in quarantine before relying on it. Never delete document history to free capacity or remove integrity holds merely to make a health check pass.

This source export does not establish that any particular business migration, recovery plan, statutory calculation or browser/device flow has been accepted. Review those explicitly before live payroll. Email, money transfer, public exposure and new account access require their own supported configuration; they are not implied by source publication.
